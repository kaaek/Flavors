Component sheet

Every building block the app needs, using the real states and wording from the spec. Styles live in components.css; see screens.html for them composed into role screens, and styleguide.html for the rules behind them.

01 · Buttons

One main action per screen

Paprika is the single call to action. Teal is for everything else that matters, outline and ghost for the rest. Labels say what happens ("Release payment"), never "Submit".

Variants
States

Disabled buttons should say why nearby ("Approval blocked: needs reconnection"). Loading buttons keep their label and set aria-busy.

Sizes

Full width on phones (diner and cashier pages).
