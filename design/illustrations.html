Illustrations and patterns

The "delicious chaos": flat food shapes, die-cut sticker outlines, and the heart-V as the one constant. All of it is plain SVG with no text inside, generated by illustrations/build-illustrations.py from the brand palette. Related: style guide · components · screens.

01 · Heart-V pattern

A quiet texture

Tone-on-tone hearts, tilted and offset like bricks. It tiles seamlessly, so use it as a CSS background-image behind heroes, banners and the diner pages. Keep it low-contrast: it must never compete with text.

pattern-teal.svg for dark teal sections.

pattern-cream.svg for the page background behind cards.

pattern-sand.svg for muted panels and the QR page.

04 · Stickers

Delicious chaos, die-cut

Our own sticker set, replacing the licensed cartoon cat from the pitch deck. Food only, no characters, each with a white outline and a soft shadow. Scatter two or three, tilted a few degrees, like they were stuck on.

stickers/*.svg, 200 × 200 each: burger, pizza, cup, fries, donut (food) · cutlery, heart, verified, qr, sparkle (brand). They read on both cream and dark teal because of the white outline. Also: confetti.svg, loose squiggles and dots to fill gaps.

05 · Using them

Where the fun stops

Yes: empty states, onboarding, success, 404, marketing, diner pagesMoments where nothing is at stake. Two or three stickers at most.
Yes: the "verified" sticker as a badgeIt is the one sticker with meaning, and it appears only on admin-approved creators.
No: payments, escrow amounts, disputes, errors, legal screensStay plain and calm (see the voice rules). The escrow spot is allowed as an explainer only.
No: on top of text, numbers, tables or buttonsStickers sit beside content, never over it.
No: new colors, faces or charactersDraw new stickers with the palette and keep them food-only. Never reuse third-party mascots.
AccessibilityIllustrations are decorative: use alt="". The meaning always lives in the text beside them.
