Brand style guide

Verified food creators, escrow you can trust, and every redemption tracked. This page is the one source for how Flavors looks and sounds.

01 · Brand

Who we are

Flavors connects F&B brands with verified food creators, holds payment safely in escrow, and uses QR codes to show brands the real offline sales a campaign produced.

Trustworthy

Money and verification are serious. Teal, clear numbers, no surprises. Escrow states are always spelled out.

Appetizing

Food is the point. Warm cream, paprika and saffron, and big honest food photography make every screen feel like a menu, not a spreadsheet.

Playful, not silly

Personality lives in illustration, empty states and celebration moments. Payment, dispute and error screens stay calm and plain.

We are not artists, but we can turn this chaos into delicious.

Brand line from the pitch. Use it for marketing, onboarding and the 404 page, not in transactional screens.

02 · Logo

The heart is the V

The heart-shaped V replaces the V in FLAVORS. It never changes color; only the lettering switches between paprika (light backgrounds) and cream (dark ones).

flavors-logo.svg on Cream, White or Sand.

flavors-logo-dark.svg on Teal 700 or darker (shown on Teal 900).

flavors-icon.svg: avatars, loading, empty states. Same heart on any background.

The icon works on dark teal too.

*-mono.svg uses currentColor: shown here in the default black. Inline the SVG in HTML to color it with any brand color; for single-color print and watermarks.

Clear space and minimum size
≥ 1/6 height
  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the heart's notch (about 1/6 of the logo height) empty on every side.
  • Minimum size: wordmark 96 px wide. Smaller than that, use the icon. Icon 24 px; smaller than that, the favicon.
  • Favicon / app icon: favicon.svg, favicon.ico, app-icon.svg (cream rounded square, PWA).
  • No stacked version: the heart is the V, so stacking would spell "FLA ORS". Square spaces use the icon.
Don't

Stretch or squash it.

Recolor it or its heart.

Put the light wordmark on teal or orange. Use the dark one.

Two rules that come from the contrast numbers

White text on Saffron fails (2.8:1): Saffron fills take Ink text. Saffron on a light background fails too (2.6:1): it is decorative only, never text. When you need orange text, use Saffron 700.

04 · Typography

Marcellus and Lato

Marcellus is the closest free match to the light, flared lettering in the logo, so it is used big and sparingly. Lato does all the work: paragraphs, buttons, forms, tables.

06 · Voice

Warm, clear, never cute about money

Talk like a friendly host who also happens to be good with numbers. Short sentences. Say what happened and what's next.

DoPayment of $500 is held safely in escrow. It's released when you approve the creator's proof.
Don'tYour transaction has been processed successfully.
DoNo redemptions yet. Once diners scan your QR code, they'll show up here.
Don'tOops!! Nothing here 🍕🍕
DoWe couldn't verify this account. Check the profile link and try again.
Don'tUh oh, we're not feeling the vibes of that link 😬
  • Personality belongs in: empty states, onboarding, success moments, marketing.
  • Plain language belongs in: payments, escrow, disputes, errors, anything legal. No emoji, no food puns.
  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons ("Approve proof", not "Approve Proof").
  • Say what the button does ("Release payment"), not "Submit" or "OK".
  • Money: currency symbol first, two decimals, thousands separators: $1,250.00.
07 · Imagery and icons

Real food, simple shapes

Photography

Real food, real people, real tables: like the pitch. Warm light, close crops, creators mid-bite. No stock-handshake office photos.

Illustration

Flat shapes with the brand palette and the heart-V motif. Friendly, a little chaotic ("delicious chaos"), always on Cream or white. See illustrations.html.

Icons

Outline icons, 2 px stroke, rounded caps and joins, drawn on a 24 px grid. Teal by default, Paprika for the single main action. Always paired with a text label for anything that moves money.

Third-party characters

The pitch deck uses a licensed cartoon cat mascot as decoration. It is not part of the Flavors identity and must not ship in the product. Our own food-only sticker set replaces it (see it).
